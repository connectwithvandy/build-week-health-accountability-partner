## About

Your meals, movement, water, and plans, remembered.

## Business description

Tell Ted what you ate, how you moved, how much water you drank, and what you planned to do. Ted keeps track as the day unfolds, gives you one useful nudge when the day slips, and sends an evening recap so you can close the day honestly.
