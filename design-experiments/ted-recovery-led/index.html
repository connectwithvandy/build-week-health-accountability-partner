Before you start

Health conversations are personal.

Ted stores your profile, messages, plans, logs, and uploaded media so it can remember your day. Ted and the services that run it process this information.

To remove it, message “delete my data” in WhatsApp and confirm the request.

Ted is for adults 18+ during beta. It is a habit coach, not medical advice, and should not be used for emergencies, diagnosis, or treatment.
