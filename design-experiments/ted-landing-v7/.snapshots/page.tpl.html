Ask for the nudge. Ask it to back off.

Supplements, water, the 4 p.m. walk, the thing you keep meaning to do — tell Ted in a sentence and it holds the time, not you.

Say you are travelling, ill, or just done for the day and it goes quiet. Quiet hours are yours to set. Ted stays silent by default and only speaks when it has earned the interruption — a companion that over-pings gets muted.
