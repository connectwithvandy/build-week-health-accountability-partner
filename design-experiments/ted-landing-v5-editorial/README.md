# Ted landing — v5, editorial

A TBH-derived editorial layout, rebuilt 2 Sep 2026.

Preview:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 4180 --directory design-experiments/ted-landing-v5-editorial
```

`ted-landing.html` is one self-contained file — CSS, fonts link, and both photos inlined.
`ted-landing.artifact.html` is the identical file, kept for publishing.

Nothing here is imported by `src/app`. It creates no route and cannot affect the live site.

## What it is

Cool white ground with film grain, near-black full-bleed bands, and orange as the only colour
on the page. Fraunces 900 for display, Outfit for everything under 32px, Caveat as the brush
accent on the last phrase of each headline, Baloo 2 for the wordmark.

Copy comes from heyted.vercel.app. Chat threads are real — the hero exchange is verbatim from a
live WhatsApp log, the rest is drawn from `hermes/SOUL.md`.

## The things worth knowing

**Scroll is time.** Nothing on this page runs on a clock. Every thread is scrubbed by the
scrollbar: move down and the messages send, move back up and they un-send. The gap between two
messages holds the real waiting state — Ted's typing dots if the next line is his, or your own
words appearing in the composer a character at a time if the next line is yours.

A pill rides with you showing the clock moving forward as you pass each section, because the
page is one day: 3:00 PM → 7:42 PM → 9:12 PM → 10:45 PM, then it leaves when the day is over.

Sections arrive with the same easing a WhatsApp bubble has when it leaves your thumb
(`--send`), so the page assembles the way a thread does. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the
threads render complete and the pill is hidden.

Because the sections ARE the clock, they must stay in time order. `#nudge` (7:42 PM) comes
before `#ask` (9:12 PM). Reordering them breaks the concept, not just the layout.

**The numbers tie across the page.** The 3:00 PM meal photo puts the day at 49g protein over
2 meals; the 7:42 PM nudge closes 2,100 steps to land on 8,214; the gym is moved to Saturday,
not deleted; the 10:45 PM review reads all three back. Change one and change the rest.

SOUL.md's wrap-up example (540 kcal / 4,863 steps) contradicted the meal photo, so the review
uses 1,180 kcal / 8,214 steps instead. The voice is verbatim; only the arithmetic moved.

**The WhatsApp chrome is drawn, not screenshotted** — iOS status bar, back chevron and unread
badge, avatar, call icons, composer, and a doodle wallpaper generated by `doodles.py` in the
build scratch. Timestamps float right on the last line, as WhatsApp's really do; on a photo they
sit on the image under a scrim.

**Two meal photos, never repeated.** The paneer-and-rajma plate in the hero thread, a dosa in
the "Photo" format card.

## Known gaps

- Mobile has breakpoints written but has not been looked at in a narrow viewport.
- Three sections share the same shape (paragraph beside a chat card); by the third it flattens.
- Section headings are small relative to the hero.
- The footer is the only part that has not been designed.

## Rebuilding

`ted-landing.html` is generated, not hand-edited. The source is in `build/`:

```bash
cd build && python3 build.py     # writes preview-white.html
cp preview-white.html ../ted-landing.html
cp preview-white.html ../ted-landing.artifact.html
```

`page.tpl.html` is the template, `build.py` injects the palette, the WhatsApp chrome and
the two meal photos as base64, and `doodles.py` generates the chat wallpaper. Edit the
template — never the generated file, it will be overwritten.
